## Overview

A premium glassmorphic Progressive Web App centered on a smooth, liquid dark aesthetic. Three utility modes — clock, timer, stopwatch — wrapped in polished animations and full offline support.

## Features

- **Clock** — 12h/24h toggle, animated gooey canvas background
- **Timer** — Countdown with custom input
- **Stopwatch** — Lap tracking with CSV export
- **PWA** — Installable on desktop and mobile, fully offline via service worker
- **Fullscreen mode** — Screensaver mode toggled by `F` key, controls auto-hide on inactivity
- **Persistence** — All settings and lap history saved to localStorage

## Design

Liquid glossy dark design system with a Canvas-based animated background — interactive particle effects that respond to cursor movement.
