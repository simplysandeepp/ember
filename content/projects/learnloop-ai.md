## Overview

An adaptive learning platform engineered to match educational content to individual learning styles. Built for focus — premium glassmorphism design, intelligent assistance, and detailed performance analytics.

## Key Features

- **AI Chat Assistant** — Instant subject help, concept explanation, and clarification on complex topics
- **Performance Analytics** — Dashboard tracking study sessions, time spent, and learning progress
- **Study Planner** — Schedule management, learning goals, and reminders
- **Weak Area Analysis** — Identifies specific topics the student struggles with and surfaces them for focused review
